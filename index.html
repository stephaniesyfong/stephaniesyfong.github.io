About

I am a first-year PhD student at the Monash AIM for Health Lab, supervised by A/Prof. Dominic Dwyer, A/Prof. Zongyuan Ge, and A/Prof. Vincent Lee. I am supported by a full scholarship through the CSIRO NextGen Graduates Program and also work as a research assistant at Orygen.

With a background in psychology, my research sits at the intersection of mental health, natural language processing, and responsible AI. I am particularly interested in how language and other behavioural signals can be used to understand psychological states, and how AI systems can be made more clinically useful, interpretable, safe, and sensitive to the social contexts in which they are deployed.

Research Interests

Clinical & mental-health NLP Explainable & responsible AI LLM safety, ethics & fairness Stigma & bias evaluation Concept-based & interpretable models Multimodal mental-health screening
